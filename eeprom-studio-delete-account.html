Delete your EEPROM Studio account

EEPROM Studio users can request deletion of their app account and associated account data using the options below. This page is publicly accessible without signing in.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open EEPROM Studio on your Android device.
  2. Open Account & subscription and sign in if needed.
  3. Select Options, then Delete app account.
  4. Read the confirmation and select Delete app account to submit the deletion request.

The account-deletion action is intended to stop renewal of the linked Stripe subscription, delete the EEPROM Studio account profile, and revoke its login sessions. It does not delete your Google account.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email marjiqussai14@gmail.com using the email address associated with your EEPROM Studio account. Use the subject EEPROM Studio Account Deletion Request and ask us to delete your account and associated personal data. We may need to confirm that you own the account before processing your request.

Request account deletion by email

What happens to your data?

  • Account data: The app's account-deletion process removes the stored Google account identity (account identifier, email, and display name) and revokes linked sign-in sessions.
  • Subscription: The linked Stripe recurring renewal is stopped through the app's account-deletion process. Any separate subscriptions with other providers must be managed separately.
  • Payment records: Certain invoices, transaction details, and records required for accounting, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or disputes may be retained for the applicable required periods. Stripe may independently retain payment information under its own policies.
  • Local files: EEPROM dumps, backups and module files stored on your phone are not automatically removed by deleting your online app account. Delete those files using your device's file manager if you no longer want them.

If you want to request deletion of specific account information without closing your account, or ask about retained records, contact the same support email. We will review requests under applicable law.
