EEPROM Studio · Car Key Fob & ECU

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EEPROM Studio, operated by Car Key Fob & ECU (“we,” “our”), handles information when you use the EEPROM Studio Android application and related account and payment services.

At a glance: EEPROM, MCU and vehicle-module files you open, read, edit or save in the app remain on your device during normal use. The app also has optional online account and paid-feature services that process a limited amount of account, session and billing information. Local file storage does not mean that no account data is processed online.

1. Data stored on your device

EEPROM Studio can read, display, edit and save data from supported automotive programmer hardware, EEPROM chips and vehicle modules. This data can include binary dumps, module configuration, VIN-related data and other technical identifiers contained in user-selected files. Such files, backups and edits are handled on your device, and are not automatically uploaded to the EEPROM Studio account or billing server.

Files may remain in app storage or in a location you choose on your device. If you export or share a file using another app or cloud-storage provider, that destination handles it under its own practices. Uninstalling the app or deleting its local files may permanently remove those files; keep your own backups.

2. Account information

When you choose to sign in to access account-linked features, we use Google sign-in. Our account service may receive and store your Google account's unique identifier, verified email address and display name. We use this information to identify your account, restore your membership across devices, and answer support or account requests. Your Google password is not provided to us.

Our service also creates account identifiers, short-lived sign-in information and revocable session records. The app stores session information on the device to keep you signed in. The account service uses a hosted database to store the information needed to authenticate users and check membership eligibility.

3. Payment and subscription information

When you purchase or manage a paid feature using Stripe, checkout and payment-method collection take place through Stripe's hosted pages. Stripe processes information necessary for payments, such as billing contact and payment details, subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card number in EEPROM Studio's account database.

Our membership service stores or receives records needed to verify and administer your purchase, such as Stripe customer or subscription identifiers, transaction or checkout identifiers, membership status, paid-period dates, and billing events. We use these records to enable or disable paid features, prevent duplicate entitlements, provide customer support, and manage cancellations or account deletion.

If an alternative payment flow is offered in a particular app distribution, its payment provider may process the information necessary to complete that transaction; the applicable checkout will identify the provider.

4. Technical and network information

When the app connects to the sign-in, membership or payment service, standard internet connections may expose technical information such as IP address, request time and basic request metadata to the service provider. This information can be processed for service operation, reliability, troubleshooting, security and abuse prevention. The app may also access connected USB programmer hardware and local files at your direction.

Where app updates or downloads use an external service, that service may process technical request information under its own privacy policy.

5. How information is used

  • To run EEPROM Studio's local programming and file tools.
  • To authenticate signed-in users and protect their sessions.
  • To confirm purchases and control access to paid app features.
  • To respond to support requests and help resolve billing or account issues.
  • To maintain security, investigate abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use the contents of your EEPROM files for targeted advertising.

6. Service providers and sharing

We use service providers only as needed to provide the features described above, including:

  • Google for sign-in and identity verification.
  • Stripe for hosted checkout, recurring billing and payment administration.
  • Cloudflare for hosting our membership service and its account database.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this public Privacy Policy webpage; GitHub may process ordinary website access information.

These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect users and our services.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for online account and payment communications, hashed server-side session identifiers and device security features for local session storage. Payment card entry takes place with the payment processor rather than in EEPROM Studio. No internet-connected system is guaranteed to be completely secure, so we encourage users to secure their devices and avoid sharing sensitive module files unnecessarily.

8. Retention and deletion

Local EEPROM files and backups remain where you save them until you remove them using the app, your file manager or device controls. Deleting an online account does not automatically remove user-managed local files.

Account and membership data is kept while needed to provide the service. You can request deletion using Account & subscription → Delete app account within the app, if that option is available in your installed version, or by emailing the contact below. Account deletion is intended to remove your EEPROM Studio account profile and revoke linked sessions; cancellation of any active recurring subscription is handled as part of the account-deletion workflow. Billing-related records may need to be retained by Stripe or by us where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

To ask what information we hold, request correction, or request deletion, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting on the request.

9. Children

EEPROM Studio is a professional automotive electronics tool and is not designed or directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published. Any new feature that transfers EEPROM files to our servers will require a corresponding update to this disclosure before launch.
